# IELTS Study Tracker

A one-teacher, one-student coaching tracker that takes an Academic IELTS candidate from around band 5.0 to 6.0+ overall over a roughly six-month course, putting most effort into Listening and Reading (80/20).

## Language

### Course structure

**Course**:
The whole coaching programme for one student, running from a start date to a target end date (about six months, moved to the exam date once booked).
_Avoid_: Program, plan

**Skill track**:
The path one IELTS skill (Listening, Reading or Writing) takes through its own sequence of Stages, independent of the other skills.
_Avoid_: Subject, module

**Stage**:
One phase of a Skill track with a single focus, a few Goals and a small Habit list; a track is in exactly one Stage at a time.
_Avoid_: Phase, level, week

**Gate**:
The condition for leaving a Stage, usually a test score; when it is met the app suggests advancing, and the teacher confirms.
_Avoid_: Milestone, checkpoint, unlock

**Goal**:
A one-off piece of work that must be finished within a Stage, such as finishing a book's Listening chapters or sitting a Full test; it may carry a due date.
_Avoid_: Mission, task, objective, homework

**Habit**:
A recurring practice the student repeats on a fixed rhythm (daily or so many times a week) for as long as its Stage lasts, such as daily BBC Learning English or dictation twice a week.
_Avoid_: Routine, daily task, mission

### Teaching content

**Lilie technique**:
The teacher's exam-taking method for Listening and Reading, taught once Foundation is finished.
_Avoid_: Lilie way, test strategy

**Foundation**:
The first Stage of the Listening and Reading tracks: working through the matching chapters of "The Official Cambridge Guide to IELTS".
_Avoid_: Basics, beginner stage

**Diagnosis**:
The Stage right after Foundation in which the student sits a first Full test and learns the Lilie technique, to reveal what she is weak at.
_Avoid_: Placement, assessment stage

**Focus stage**:
The Stage after Diagnosis spent fixing weaknesses: Sections in order 1 to 4 (skipping strong ones) for Listening, weakest Question types for Reading; its Gate is a Full test of 7.0 or above.
_Avoid_: Improvement stage, drill stage

**Exam-ready stage**:
The last Stage of the Listening track, after its Focus Gate is passed: Habits and a Full test every two months to hold the level.
_Avoid_: Maintenance, final stage

**Passage 3 stage**:
The last Stage of the Reading track, after its Focus Gate is passed: practice on Passage 3 only until the exam.
_Avoid_: Final stage, advanced stage

**Templates stage**:
The first Stage of the Writing track, starting after both Foundations: learning one Task 1 template (bar and line charts) and one Task 2 template.
_Avoid_: Writing foundation

**Practice stage**:
The Writing Stage after Templates, lasting until the exam: marked pieces with about one third of Writing time on Task 1 and two thirds on Task 2.
_Avoid_: Writing drills

### The test

**Full test**:
A complete, timed IELTS paper for one skill, taken in Diagnosis and then every two months.
_Avoid_: Mock, practice test, final test

**Section**:
One of the four parts of the Listening test (Section 1 to Section 4).
_Avoid_: Part

**Passage**:
One of the three texts of the Academic Reading test (Passage 1 to Passage 3).
_Avoid_: Text, part

**Question type**:
A recurring IELTS question format such as Matching headings or Yes / No / Not Given; Reading weaknesses are tracked by Question type, not by Passage.
_Avoid_: Task type, exercise type
